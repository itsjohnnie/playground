The three little piggies

a scroll-along storybook

A scroll-driven storybook rendered in ASCII characters: three little pigs leave home, build houses of straw, sticks and bricks, and a wolf huffs and puffs as you scroll — the straw and stick houses blow apart, the brick house holds, the wolf falls down the chimney into hot soup and runs away, and the pigs dance under fireworks. Scroll to advance; the credits below the animation have links and two interactive pieces.

$ story --complete

And they all lived happily ever after

Every pig, every house and every huff you just scrolled through was computed live from characters: no video, no images. The wind was your thumb the whole time. Two little toys below, to keep playing.
